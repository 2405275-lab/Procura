# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
Veridion is an Enterprise Procurement Decision Intelligence Platform that automates the quotation review lifecycle using a multi-agent AI architecture.

## Target Audience
Procurement Directors, Finance Officers, Purchase Managers, and Compliance Auditors.

## Core Modules & Features

### 1. Purchase Request Management
- Intake budget limits, priority levels, categories, and item counts.
- Search, filter by status (Open, Draft, Under Review, Approved), and create new requisitions.

### 2. OCR & Extraction Agent Pipelines
- Accept PDF, JPG invoice files.
- Automatically parse headers, price totals, tax variables, warranties, and delivery SLA terms.

### 3. Vendor Comparison Engine
- Dynamic side-by-side matrices comparing warranty coverage, delivery days, and contract success histories.
- Radar charts representing vendor intelligence scores.

### 4. Compliance Policy Auditor
- Verify quotation inputs against active rules.
- Log override exceptions and manager justifications on immutable logs.

### 5. Purchase Order Generator & ERP Sync
- Transform approved quotations into professional, SAP-ready PO files.
